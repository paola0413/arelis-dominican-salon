How to Make Your Silk Press Last

With good care, a silk press can last up to two weeks. Wrap your hair or sleep on a silk pillowcase, avoid humidity and sweat when you can, and skip heavy oils that weigh hair down.

We protect your curl pattern by choosing the right temperature for your texture — so when you wash, your curls come back healthy and ready for your next look.
